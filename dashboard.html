      Email Reminders

      Get an email when a task is added, when one is about to start, when it's completed, or if it's overdue — sent through a free EmailJS account connected to a Gmail address. One-time setup: see the README's "Free Gmail Email Reminders" section.

      Reminders are checked about once a minute while this dashboard is open on some device (including the installed app) — there's no background server, so nothing sends while every device is fully closed. "Task added" and "task completed" emails are sent immediately and don't depend on the periodic check.
